Why Banting

The Banting lifestyle — and why tracking matters

  • ✓

    The 50g net carb rule is non-negotiable

    Banting requires keeping net carbs under 50g per day to stay in fat-burning mode. Without a tracker, hidden carbs in sauces, dressings, and packaged foods push you over without you knowing.

  • ✓

    Supermarkets are full of hidden sugars

    Food labelling can be misleading. Products marketed as "healthy" or "low fat" often contain maltodextrin, dextrose, or high fructose corn syrup. The barcode scanner catches these instantly.

  • ✓

    Meal planning is where most people fail

    Without a plan, you reach for whatever's available — which is rarely Banting-friendly. The built-in meal planner removes decision fatigue by showing you compliant meals that fit your remaining macros.

  • ✓

    Privacy matters for health data

    Your weight loss journey is personal. Unlike other diet apps that upload your data to remote servers for advertising profiling, Banting Buddy keeps everything on your device.

The Banting movement

The Banting lifestyle — also called LCHF (Low Carb High Fat) — was pioneered by Professor Tim Noakes and the Real Meal Revolution. It focuses on eliminating sugar and refined carbohydrates, eating high-quality fats, and maintaining a net carb intake under 50g per day. What started as a movement has grown into a worldwide community, with millions of followers managing their health through this low-carb approach. Banting Buddy 2.0 gives this community a purpose-built digital tool to support their journey.

What makes a good Banting app?

A good Banting app needs accurate net carb tracking (not just total carbs), a comprehensive product database for barcode scanning, offline functionality for privacy and reliability, and practical meal planning tools. Banting Buddy 2.0 delivers all of these in a single app — free to download, with no mandatory account creation, and no personal data ever leaving your device.
