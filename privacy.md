# Privacy

This site is intentionally boring about data:

- No accounts, no comments, no newsletters.
- No Google Analytics. We use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to see which pages get visited — no cross-site tracking.
- Google Play install links include a `referrer` (utm_source=banting-site) so we can see which page led to an install. That's reported in aggregate in Play Console.
- The Banting Buddy app itself works offline. Your macro logs, weight history and grocery lists stay on your device. No cloud sync, no advertising SDKs.

Contact via the Play Store listing if you want a page corrected or removed.
