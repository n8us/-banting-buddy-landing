# Banting meal plans

**Short answer:** A Banting meal plan is three meals a day built on protein, above-ground vegetables and natural fat, with no starch or sugar. Start with the [free 7-day plan]({{ site.baseurl }}/guides/7-day-banting-meal-plan/) if you are new, then use the framework below to build your own week once the first one feels normal.

The plan is the easy part. The shopping, prep and swaps are what make it stick, so every plan here includes a grocery list and a portion guide.

## What a Banting day looks like

Use the plate model instead of counting every gram:

- **Protein:** one palm-sized portion per meal — meat, fish, eggs or cheese.
- **Vegetables:** half the plate, above the ground — spinach, cabbage, cauliflower, broccoli, courgette, peppers, salad.
- **Fat:** one thumb — butter, olive oil, avocado, cream, cheese.
- **Carbs:** whatever fits your daily limit, usually berries, nuts or dairy rather than bread or rice.

A typical day lands around 30–40g net carbs without weighing anything. If you want to track, the net carb bands used on this site are: low (0–10g), medium (10–20g) and high (20g+).

## How to build your own Banting meal plan

1. **Pick three proteins for the week.** A cheap mince, a chicken cut and a fish. Add eggs and boerewors for variety.
2. **Pick two vegetables you will actually prep.** Cabbage and cauliflower are cheap, filling and keep well.
3. **Choose a fat for each meal.** Butter for veg, olive oil for salad, cream for sauce, avo for the plate.
4. **Plan breakfast once.** Eggs, yoghurt or chia — repeating breakfast removes a decision every morning.
5. **Cook double at dinner.** Tomorrow's lunch is the single biggest predictor of staying on plan.
6. **Shop once, top up once.** Buy meat and dairy for the week, fresh veg twice.
7. **Keep two emergency meals.** Biltong and cheese, or eggs and frozen spinach. No plan survives a bad day without a backup.

## A shopping list template

**Meat and fish:** eggs, beef mince, chicken thighs or breasts, hake or snoek, boerewors, bacon, biltong.
**Dairy:** butter, full-cream cream, cheddar, cream cheese, plain full-cream yoghurt.
**Vegetables:** cabbage, cauliflower, broccoli, spinach, baby marrows, peppers, mushrooms, cucumber, salad leaves, avocado, onions, garlic.
**Pantry:** olive oil, coconut cream, tinned tomatoes, tomato paste, curry powder, paprika, salt, pepper, almond flour, psyllium husk, chia seeds, macadamias.
**Drinks:** rooibos, coffee, water. No juice or cold drink.

## Meal prep in 90 minutes

1. Boil six eggs.
2. Brown 1kg mince and split it: half for bobotie, half for a tomato sauce.
3. Roast a tray of chicken thighs and a tray of vegetables.
4. Make a jar of olive-oil dressing.
5. Wash and chop salad veg into containers.
6. Bake a loaf of [Banting bread]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-bread/) or a batch of [seed crackers]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-seed-crackers/).

That is breakfast, lunch and dinner covered for three days.

## Meal plans FAQ

### How many carbs should I eat on Banting?

Most people aim for 25–50g net carbs a day. The plans here sit at 30–40g, which leaves room for dairy, nuts and berries. If you are very active or new to low-carb, start at the higher end.

### Do I have to eat breakfast?

No. Two meals and a snack work if you are not hungry. If you take medication for diabetes, discuss skipped meals with your doctor first.

### Can I follow these plans if I don't eat meat?

Yes, but you need to replace the protein with eggs, cheese, tofu, tempeh or legumes in small portions. Legumes are higher-carb, so count them. The app's recipe library includes vegetarian options.

### How much weight will I lose?

That depends on your starting point, portions and activity. Most of the first week's loss is water. A realistic rate after that is 0.5–1kg a week. The plans are a structure, not a guarantee.

### Where are the recipes?
