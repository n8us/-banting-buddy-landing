# 30-day Banting meal plan

**Short answer:** This 30-day plan repeats a four-week rotation of simple South African meals, each day landing around **30–40g net carbs**. It is designed for one shop a week, leftovers for lunch, and no ingredients you cannot find at Checkers, Pick n Pay, Woolworths or Shoprite.

Four weeks is long enough to build the habit and short enough to see whether it suits you. Do not aim for perfect. Aim for 25 days out of 30.

## The rules for the month

1. **Three meals, no grazing.** Eat until satisfied, then stop.
2. **Protein at every meal.** Palm-sized portion.
3. **Half the plate is vegetables** that grow above the ground.
4. **Fat is a ingredient, not a treat.** Butter, olive oil, cream, avocado.
5. **No sugar, grains, potatoes, rice, pasta, pap or juice.** No exceptions for the first two weeks.
6. **Cook double at dinner.** Lunch is tomorrow's dinner, or close to it.
7. **Drink water, rooibos or black coffee.** Cream is fine; sugar is not.
8. **Salt your food.** Low-carb eating flushes sodium.
9. **Walk 20 minutes a day.** Not for calories — for sleep and mood.
10. **Track if you stall.** The app makes net carbs visible without guesswork.

## Prep Sunday (90 minutes)

- Boil 6–8 eggs.
- Brown 1kg mince; split into bobotie and a tomato sauce.
- Roast a tray of chicken thighs and a tray of vegetables.
- Make a jar of olive-oil dressing.
- Chop salad vegetables into containers.
- Bake a [Banting bread]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-bread/) or [seed crackers]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-seed-crackers/).

## Eating out during the 30 days

- **Braai:** meat, wors, chicken, fish, salad, braaied veg. No pap, rolls or sweet marinade.
- **Restaurant:** order a steak, grilled fish or chicken with vegetables or salad, and ask for no chips or rice.
- **Fast food:** most burger places will serve a burger without the bun and add a salad. Avoid sugary sauces.
- **Coffee shop:** black coffee or with cream; skip the muffin and the flavoured latte.
- **Petrol station:** biltong, cheese, nuts, boiled eggs. Avoid the pie and the energy drink.

## If you stall

1. Check portions of nuts, cheese and cream — they add up fast.
2. Check "Banting" packaged products for starch and sugar.
3. Check your drinks, including alcohol and "diet" mixes.
4. Sleep and stress affect appetite more than willpower.
5. Give it two weeks before changing anything. Water weight moves first.

## 30-day meal plan FAQ

### Can I repeat week 1 for all four weeks?

Yes, but boredom is the main reason people quit. The rotation exists to keep the food interesting.

### Is this plan suitable for a family?

Yes. Cook the same protein and vegetables for everyone and add rice, pap or potatoes for family members who want it.

### Can I freeze the meals?

Most dinners freeze well: bobotie, curry, mince sauce, chicken bake and soup. Salad and fried eggs do not.

### Where are the recipes for each meal?

See the [Banting recipes hub]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/) for breakfast, dinner, snacks, desserts and braai. The app includes 200+ recipes with net carbs, plus a grocery list builder.

### What if I miss a day?

Start again at the next meal. One meal does not undo a month, and guilt is a worse motivator than consistency.
