# Banting Buddy vs other keto and carb counting apps

**Short answer:** Banting Buddy is set up for Banting from the first open: around 50g net carbs a day, green/orange/red food lists, barcode scanner and Banting meals. No macro setup, no conversions, no account.

Banting Buddy starts on Banting: net carbs with fibre shown, food lists in Banting terms, and label checks that make sense in a South African supermarket.

| | Banting Buddy 2.0 | Other keto/carb counting apps |
|---|---|---|
| Carb default | ~50g net, fibre shown | Usually needs manual setup |
| Food lists | Green, orange and red lists built in | Usually generic or keto wording |
| Barcode scan | Built in for quick net-carb checks | Varies by app, often limited |
| Offline | Logs and lists work without signal | Varies, many need data for search and sync |
| Meal ideas | Banting meals in app | Usually generic, seldom Banting-specific |
| Account | No account, stays on your phone | Usually needs an account with cloud sync |
| Cost | Free with ads, all features included; optional ad removal for 1, 3 or 12 months | Varies, check the Play Store listing in rands |

Source for carb maths: USDA FoodData Central, Sep 2026.

## Why Banting Buddy fits Banting

Setup is done for you. Your target is already around 50g net carbs with fibre subtracted, and the food language matches the [Banting food list for beginners]({{ site.baseurl }}/guides/banting-food-list-beginners/). No macro maths on day one.

Aisle checks are quick. Pull out your phone at Checkers or Pick n Pay, scan a barcode and see net carbs plus flagged sugars without signal. I use it most for peanut butter, yoghurt and sauces — the three aisles where "healthy" labels mislead fastest. Our [peanut butter question]({{ site.baseurl }}/questions/is-peanut-butter-banting-friendly/) shows how a 3g serve becomes 8g when maltodextrin sneaks in.

Private and simple. No account, no password, no food log on a server. Your logs stay on your phone.

## Getting started with Banting Buddy

Install it, log your first day against the ~50g net target, and keep [how to count net carbs]({{ site.baseurl }}/guides/how-to-count-net-carbs/) open while you shop. The most common beginner trap is tracking total carbs against a net target, which makes avo look expensive and bread look nearly fine. Our [cauliflower carbs]({{ site.baseurl }}/questions/how-many-carbs-in-cauliflower/) and [avocado carbs]({{ site.baseurl }}/questions/how-many-carbs-in-avocado/) pages show the per-100g maths you'll reuse.

When you are ready to cook, use the [7-day Banting meal plan]({{ site.baseurl }}/guides/7-day-banting-meal-plan/).
