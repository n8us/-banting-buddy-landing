**Short answer:** Hardly any — about 1.8 g net carbs per 100 g (half a large avo). In Banting (*The Real Meal Revolution*), avocado is an undisputed **Green list** staple: high in healthy monounsaturated fats, packed with fibre, and with negligible impact on blood sugar.

| Per 100 g | Total carbs | Fibre | Net carbs | Fat | Protein | Banting List |
|-----------|-------------|-------|-----------|-----|---------|--------------|
| Avocado, raw | 8.5 g | 6.7 g | 1.8 g | 15 g | 2 g | Green (Freely) |

Half a large avocado (about 100 g) has about 8.5 g total carbs, 6.7 g fibre and **1.8 g net carbs**. A whole large avo (200 g) costs only about **3.6 g net carbs**.

Source: USDA FoodData Central, Sep 2026.

## What to watch for

Avocado is a cornerstone Green list food. Almost 80% of its total carbohydrates are dietary fibre, meaning it barely impacts blood glucose. The practical considerations around avocado come down to seasonality, cost, and what you eat it with:

Price first, because SA avos swing wildly. In season (autumn and winter) local Fuerte and Hass avos drop to R10–R15 each at Checkers, Pick n Pay, and street stalls; out of season, a single imported Hass at Woolies can hit R25–R30 (seen Sep 2026). Buying a bag of six when they're firm and cheap, then fridging them as soon as they yield to gentle pressure, is the best budget trick. I checked a Woolies ripe-and-ready sticker price against a market bag in Banting Buddy's notes and the per-100 g cost differed by nearly half.

Ripeness matters for taste — rock-hard avo is bitter, overripe goes brown and stringy. Guacamole is brilliant on Banting, but watch out for restaurant servings served with corn tortilla chips (Red list). Enjoy guacamole with cucumber slices, pork crackling, or atop grilled steak or eggs.

Energy density: while avocado won't kick you out of fat-burning, it is rich in energy (~320 kcal for a whole large fruit). If your weight loss has plateaued, sticking to half an avo per meal ensures you don't crowd out protein.

## What to eat instead

You rarely need a swap — avo is the ultimate low-carb replacement for high-carb items. Use mashed avo instead of butter on banting bread, slice it into salads instead of croutons, or blend half an avo with cacao and erythritol for a rich Banting chocolate mousse. When avocados are out of season or pricey, olives and extra virgin olive oil give you similar heart-healthy monounsaturated fats.
