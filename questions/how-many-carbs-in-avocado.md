**Short answer:** Hardly any — about 1.8 g net carbs per 100 g (half a large avo). Avocado is a low-carb staple: high in healthy monounsaturated fats, packed with fibre, and with negligible impact on blood sugar.

| Per 100 g | Total carbs | Fibre | Net carbs | Fat | Protein | Carb band |
|-----------|-------------|-------|-----------|-----|---------|--------------|
| Avocado, raw | 8.5 g | 6.7 g | 1.8 g | 15 g | 2 g | Low-carb |

Half a large avocado (about 100 g) has about 8.5 g total carbs, 6.7 g fibre and **1.8 g net carbs**. A whole large avo (200 g) costs only about **3.6 g net carbs**.

Source: USDA FoodData Central.

## What to watch for

Avocado is a cornerstone low-carb food. Almost 80% of its total carbohydrates are dietary fibre, meaning it barely impacts blood glucose. The practical considerations around avocado come down to seasonality, cost, and what you eat it with:

Price first, because SA avos swing wildly. Avocado prices swing wildly with season, so buy a bag when they're ripe and cheap and fridge them as they soften. Buying a bag of six when they're firm and cheap, then fridging them as soon as they yield to gentle pressure, is the best budget trick.

Ripeness matters for taste — rock-hard avo is bitter, overripe goes brown and stringy. Guacamole is brilliant on Banting, but watch out for restaurant servings served with corn tortilla chips (high-carb). Enjoy guacamole with cucumber slices, pork crackling, or atop grilled steak or eggs.

Energy density: while avocado won't kick you out of fat-burning, it is rich in energy (~320 kcal for a whole large fruit). If your weight loss has plateaued, sticking to half an avo per meal ensures you don't crowd out protein.

## What to eat instead

You rarely need a swap — avo is a great low-carb replacement for high-carb items. Use mashed avo instead of butter on banting bread, slice it into salads instead of croutons, or blend half an avo with cacao and erythritol for a rich Banting chocolate mousse. When avocados are out of season or pricey, olives and extra virgin olive oil give you similar heart-healthy monounsaturated fats.
