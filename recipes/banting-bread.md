# Banting bread recipe

**Short answer:** This almond flour Banting bread uses psyllium husk for structure and eggs for lift, with no yeast and no wheat. One loaf gives 12 slices at roughly **2g net carbs and 165 calories a slice**. It toasts, freezes and holds a sandwich.

Most low-carb bread fails because it has nothing to hold it together. Almond flour has no gluten, so psyllium husk does that job. Do not skip it, and do not substitute coconut flour 1:1 — it absorbs far more liquid.

## Ingredients

- 200g almond flour (about 2 cups)
- 30g psyllium husk (about 4 tbsp)
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp fine salt
- 4 large eggs
- 60g butter, melted (or coconut oil)
- 120ml water
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp sesame or sunflower seeds, to top (optional)

## Method

1. **Heat the oven** to 180°C and line a 20cm x 10cm loaf tin with baking paper.
2. **Mix the dry ingredients.** Whisk the almond flour, psyllium husk, baking powder and salt in a large bowl until there are no lumps.
3. **Mix the wet ingredients.** In a jug, whisk the eggs, melted butter, water and vinegar.
4. **Combine.** Pour the wet into the dry and stir hard for about 30 seconds. The dough will be thick and slightly sticky, like a heavy muffin batter.
5. **Rest.** Leave it for 10 minutes so the psyllium can absorb the liquid. It will firm up noticeably.
6. **Shape and top.** Spoon into the tin, smooth the top with a wet spatula and scatter over the seeds.
7. **Bake** for 45–50 minutes, until the top is deep golden and a skewer comes out clean.
8. **Cool completely** in the tin for 15 minutes, then turn out onto a rack. Do not slice warm or it will tear.

## Why this recipe works

- **Psyllium husk** is the gluten replacement. It gels with water and gives the loaf elasticity, so it slices instead of crumbling.
- **Four eggs** provide lift and structure. Three eggs makes a flatter, denser loaf.
- **Vinegar** neutralises the eggy taste and helps the loaf rise.
- **Resting the dough** is not optional. If you bake immediately, the psyllium has not hydrated and the centre stays wet.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Wet centre | Bake 10 minutes longer at 170°C, or reduce the water by 20ml next time |
| Crumbly slices | Add 1 tbsp psyllium and 1 egg; slice thinner when fully cool |
| Tastes eggy | Add another teaspoon of vinegar or a tablespoon of nutritional yeast |
| Flat loaf | Check the baking powder is fresh; whisk the eggs until frothy |
| Green flecks | Sunflower seeds can react with baking soda; use baking powder only |

## Variations

- **Cheese bread:** fold in 80g grated cheddar and 1 tsp mustard powder.
- **Seed loaf:** add 50g sunflower, 30g pumpkin and 20g flaxseeds; increase water by 30ml.
- **Garlic and herb:** add 2 crushed garlic cloves, 1 tsp oregano and 1 tbsp chopped parsley.
- **Sweeter loaf:** add 2 tbsp erythritol and 1 tsp cinnamon for a breakfast bread.

## Storage

- **Counter:** 2 days in an airtight container, sliced.
- **Fridge:** up to 5 days; toast before eating.
- **Freezer:** slice, freeze flat, then toast from frozen. Keeps for 2 months.
- **Revive:** sprinkle with water and toast, or fry in butter.

## What to eat with it

Butter, cream cheese, avocado, cheddar, smoked salmon, egg mayo, biltong or liver pâté. Avoid jam and honey — that is where the carbs come back. For more loaves, see the [Banting bread recipes]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/bread/).

## FAQ

### Can I use coconut flour instead of almond flour?

Not 1:1. Coconut flour absorbs about four times its weight in liquid. Use 60–80g coconut flour and add 3 extra eggs; the result is denser. Follow the [coconut flour bread recipe]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/bread/#2-coconut-flour-bread) instead.

### Why is my Banting bread wet in the middle?

Either the loaf was sliced warm, the water was over-measured, or it needed another 10 minutes. Cool it completely, then toast the slices — a wet centre is still edible, just not sandwich-ready.

### Is Banting bread keto?

Yes, at about 2g net carbs a slice it fits both Banting and keto. It is calorie-dense, so portion matters more than the carb count.
