# Banting breakfast recipes

**Short answer:** A Banting breakfast is built around eggs, full-fat dairy, fish or last night's leftovers, with no cereal, toast or fruit juice. The eight recipes below take 3–30 minutes and land between 1g and 10g net carbs a serving.

If you are new to this, read the [Banting food list for beginners]({{ site.baseurl }}/guides/banting-food-list-beginners/) first, then come back. Every recipe uses ingredients from a normal South African supermarket.

## 1. Bacon, eggs and avocado {#bacon-eggs-and-avocado}

**Serves 1 · 10 minutes · ~2g net carbs**

The baseline Banting breakfast. Two eggs, two rashers of bacon and half an avocado.

- 2 eggs
- 2 rashers streaky bacon (no sugar or honey cure)
- ½ avocado
- 1 tsp butter or bacon fat
- Salt, black pepper, a squeeze of lemon

Fry the bacon until crisp, then fry the eggs in the rendered fat. Slide onto a plate with the avocado, season and add lemon so the avo does not taste flat. Check the bacon label: most supermarket bacon lists sugar, but under 1g per two rashers is fine.

## 2. Banting pancakes

**Serves 2 · 15 minutes · ~2g net carbs a serving**

Cream cheese and egg pancakes with no flour at all. They are soft, slightly tangy and work with butter and a few berries.

- 2 eggs
- 60g full-fat cream cheese, softened
- 1 tsp psyllium husk (optional, for structure)
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- Butter for frying

Whisk everything until smooth, rest for 5 minutes, then cook small pancakes in butter over medium-low heat. Flip once. Get the [full Banting pancake recipe]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-pancakes/) with toppings and troubleshooting.

## 3. Chia pudding with coconut milk

**Serves 2 · 5 minutes + overnight · ~3g net carbs a serving**

- 30g chia seeds
- 250ml full-fat coconut milk (or half cream, half water)
- ½ tsp vanilla
- Pinch of salt
- 40g raspberries or strawberries

Stir the chia into the coconut milk, add vanilla and salt, and refrigerate overnight. Top with berries in the morning. The fibre in chia is the point: 30g has about 12g total carbs but 10g fibre, so only around 2g net.

## 4. Cheese and mushroom omelette {#cheese-and-mushroom-omelette}

**Serves 1 · 10 minutes · ~2.5g net carbs**

- 3 eggs
- 30g cheddar or gruyère, grated
- 4 button mushrooms, sliced
- 1 tsp butter
- Salt, pepper, chopped chives

Fry the mushrooms in butter until golden, pour over the beaten eggs, scatter cheese on one half and fold when just set. Three eggs and 30g cheese give you around 27g protein, which is why it holds you until lunch.

## 5. Grain-free Banting granola with yoghurt {#grain-free-granola}

**Makes 8 servings · 30 minutes · ~10g net carbs a serving with yoghurt (medium-carb)**

Store-bought granola is not Banting. Even the "healthy" bags are built on rolled oats, dried fruit, honey or sugar syrup and vegetable oil — typically 55–65g carbs per 100g, before you add milk or yoghurt.

This recipe is different. It contains **no grain at all** — only nuts, seeds and coconut, held together with butter and cinnamon. It is still calorie-dense and the nuts carry carbs, so the portion is 30g, not a cereal-bowl fill.

- 100g almonds, roughly chopped
- 50g pecans
- 50g desiccated coconut
- 30g pumpkin seeds
- 20g chia seeds
- 30g butter or coconut oil, melted
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 2 tbsp erythritol (optional)
- 120g plain full-cream yoghurt per serving

Mix the dry ingredients, toss with melted butter and cinnamon, and bake at 160°C for 15–18 minutes, stirring once. Cool completely before storing in a jar. Serve 30g with 120g plain yoghurt and 20g blueberries — about 10g net carbs, so count it as a medium-carb breakfast rather than a free one.

## 6. Egg muffin cups

**Makes 6 · 25 minutes · ~1.5g net carbs each**

Batch-cook these on Sunday and eat them cold or reheated all week.

- 6 eggs
- 100g cooked bacon or boerewors, chopped
- 60g cheddar, grated
- ½ red pepper, diced
- Handful spinach, chopped
- Salt, pepper

Beat the eggs, stir in the fillings, pour into a greased muffin tin and bake at 180°C for 18–20 minutes until set. They keep for four days in the fridge.

## 7. Smoked mackerel with cucumber

**Serves 1 · 5 minutes · ~1g net carbs**

- 100g smoked mackerel
- ½ cucumber, sliced
- 2 tbsp full-fat sour cream or cream cheese
- Lemon, black pepper, dill if you have it

No cooking, no pan. Check the mackerel for a sweet glaze; plain smoked or peppered is the safe choice. This is also a good lunch when you are bored of eggs.

## 8. Full-cream yoghurt with berries and pecans

**Serves 1 · 3 minutes · ~9g net carbs**

- 150g plain full-cream yoghurt (double-cream, not "Greek-style" low-fat)
- 50g strawberries or raspberries
- 20g pecan nuts
- Cinnamon

Use plain yoghurt and add your own berries. Flavoured yoghurt can carry 12–15g sugar per tub. Blueberries are higher than strawberries, so keep the portion to 50g.

## What to drink

Water, rooibos, black coffee, or coffee with a splash of cream. Fruit juice, flavoured lattes, smoothies and "Banting" hot chocolate mixes are usually sugar. If you use a bulletproof coffee, count it as fat, not as a meal — it has no protein.

## Breakfasts to skip

- Cereal, muesli, granola from a box (oats, dried fruit, sugar, seed oil), oats and pronutro
- Toast, croissants, muffins, rusks made with wheat
- Fruit juice and dried fruit
- Flavoured yoghurt, drinking yoghurt and smoothies
- "Low-fat" or "lite" anything — the fat is usually replaced with starch or sugar

## Is granola Banting?

**Store-bought granola: no.** Rolled oats, dried fruit, honey, golden syrup and vegetable oil put most bags at 55–65g carbs per 100g. A 50g "healthy" serving is 25g+ carbs before milk.

**Grain-free granola: yes, in a small portion.** Nuts, seeds, coconut and butter contain no grain. But nuts are not carb-free — the recipe above is about 10g net carbs per 30g serving with yoghurt, so it is a medium-carb breakfast, not a free food.

The test is the ingredient list, not the word "granola" on the pack. If oats, raisins, honey or glucose syrup appear, it is a cereal, not a Banting food. See the [Banting food list for beginners]({{ site.baseurl }}/guides/banting-food-list-beginners/) for the full green/red breakdown.
