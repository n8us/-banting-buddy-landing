# Banting chocolate mousse recipe

**Short answer:** This Banting chocolate mousse is 85% dark chocolate folded through whipped cream, with cocoa and a pinch of salt. It serves four at about **4g net carbs a serving**, takes 20 minutes, and needs no eggs, gelatine or cooking.

It is the dessert that convinces people low-carb eating is not a punishment. The key is choosing real dark chocolate and not over-sweetening it.

## Ingredients

- 200ml double cream (or whipping cream)
- 50g 85% dark chocolate, chopped
- 1 tbsp cocoa powder
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of fine salt
- 1 tbsp erythritol (optional)

To serve: a few raspberries, a spoon of whipped cream, or a dusting of cocoa.

## Method

1. **Melt the chocolate** in a heatproof bowl over a pot of barely simmering water, or in the microwave in 20-second bursts. Remove and cool for 2 minutes.
2. **Whip the cream** to soft peaks. Stop before it gets stiff — over-whipped cream makes a grainy mousse.
3. **Fold in the flavour.** Add the cocoa, vanilla, salt and sweetener to the chocolate, then fold in one-third of the cream to loosen it.
4. **Fold in the rest** of the cream gently, using a spatula and cutting down and around the bowl. Stop as soon as there are no streaks.
5. **Divide** into four glasses and chill for at least 2 hours.
6. **Serve** with raspberries or a dusting of cocoa.

## Why it works

- **85% chocolate** has enough cocoa solids and cocoa butter to set when cold, with far less sugar than milk or 70% chocolate.
- **Soft peaks** trap air, which is the whole texture of a mousse. Stiff cream folds badly.
- **Salt** makes the chocolate taste darker and sweeter without adding sugar.
- **Cooled chocolate** is essential. Hot chocolate seizes the cream and turns it grainy.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Grainy texture | Chocolate was too hot or the cream was over-whipped |
| Mousse is too soft | Chill longer; or fold in 1 tbsp mascarpone |
| Too bitter | Add another teaspoon of erythritol, not sugar |
| Splits | Whisk in 1 tbsp cold cream to bring it back |

## Variations

- **Mocha:** add 1 tsp espresso powder to the melted chocolate.
- **Orange:** add the zest of half an orange.
- **Nutty:** fold in 30g toasted chopped hazelnuts.
- **White chocolate:** use a sugar-free white chocolate, but check the label — many are high-carb.
- **Frozen:** spoon into a loaf tin and freeze for a dense ice cream.

## Storage

Keep covered in the fridge for up to 3 days. It does not freeze well once assembled, but the frozen version above works.

## Is chocolate Banting?

Dark chocolate at 85% or higher is. A 20g square is about 3g net carbs. Milk chocolate, 70% chocolate in quantity, and anything with added sugar or maltitol is not. Check total carbs and sugar, not just the cocoa percentage.
