# Banting pizza base recipe

**Short answer:** This Banting pizza base is a fathead dough: melted mozzarella, cream cheese, egg and almond flour. It makes one large base, **8 slices at about 2g net carbs each**, and takes 30 minutes including the bake. It rolls, stretches and crisps like a proper pizza.

The toppings decide whether the meal stays Banting. Tomato paste, cheese, mushrooms, peppers, olives and salami are fine. Sweet chilli sauce, pineapple and a thick BBQ base are not.

## At a glance

| Prep time | 15 minutes |
| Bake | 12–15 minutes + 5–8 minutes with toppings |

## Ingredients

**Base**

- 170g low-moisture mozzarella, grated
- 60g full-fat cream cheese
- 1 large egg
- 100g almond flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp fine salt
- 1 tsp dried oregano

**Topping**

- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 150g mozzarella, grated
- 50g cheddar, grated
- Mushrooms, peppers, olives, salami, fresh basil
- Olive oil, salt, pepper

## Method

1. **Heat the oven** to 200°C and line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. **Melt the cheese.** Put the mozzarella and cream cheese in a microwave-safe bowl and heat in 30-second bursts until melted and stirrable. Do not let it boil.
3. **Add the egg** and stir quickly so it does not cook in the hot cheese.
4. **Add the dry ingredients** — almond flour, baking powder, salt and oregano. Mix into a soft dough.
5. **Roll it out.** Place the dough between two sheets of baking paper and roll to about 5mm thick. Lift onto the tray and remove the top sheet.
6. **Bake the base** for 12–15 minutes until golden and firm. Prick any large bubbles with a fork.
7. **Add toppings.** Spread tomato paste, scatter cheese and add your toppings. Drizzle with olive oil.
8. **Bake again** for 5–8 minutes until the cheese bubbles. Finish with fresh basil.

## Why fathead dough works

The melted cheese replaces gluten. When it cools, it holds the dough together and gives the crust a chewy bite. Almond flour adds structure and stops it turning into a cheese puddle. The egg binds the fat so it does not split.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Dough is sticky | Wet your hands, or chill it for 10 minutes |
| Base is soggy | Bake the base longer before adding toppings; drain wet toppings |
| Crust tastes eggy | Add another teaspoon of oregano and a pinch of garlic powder |
| Cheese splits in the microwave | Lower the power and stir every 20 seconds |

## Topping ideas that stay Banting

- **Margherita:** tomato paste, mozzarella, basil, olive oil.
- **Meat:** salami, boerewors crumbles, bacon, cheddar.
- **Vegetarian:** mushrooms, peppers, olives, red onion, feta.
- **Chicken:** cooked chicken, cream cheese, spinach, garlic.
- **Breakfast pizza:** scrambled egg, bacon, cheddar, chives.

Salami, ham and bacon are processed meats. Many are cured with dextrose or sugar, so read the ingredients and choose a plain dry-cured version where you can.

Avoid pineapple, sweet chilli sauce, BBQ sauce, honey-glazed ham and thick balsamic glaze.

## Storage

Wrap leftover slices and refrigerate for 3 days. Reheat in a hot oven or dry pan, not the microwave, or the base goes rubbery. Freeze baked bases between sheets of paper for up to 2 months.

## FAQ

### How many carbs in a Banting pizza?

The base is about 2g net carbs a slice. With tomato, cheese and low-carb vegetables, a quarter of the pizza lands around 10–12g net carbs. That makes it a medium-carb meal, not an everyday one.

### Can I make the base without a microwave?

Yes. Melt the cheeses in a bowl over a pot of simmering water, then remove from the heat before adding the egg.

### Why is my fathead dough grainy?

The mozzarella was not melted enough, or the almond flour was added while the cheese was too hot. Melt fully, stir smooth, then add the dry ingredients.
