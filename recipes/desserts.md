# Banting dessert recipes

**Short answer:** A Banting dessert uses cream, eggs, 85% dark chocolate, nuts, berries and a sugar-free sweetener instead of sugar and wheat flour. The eight recipes below land between 2g and 7g net carbs a serving — dessert-sized, not free-for-all.

A word on sweeteners: erythritol and xylitol do not raise blood sugar like sugar, but they are not calorie-free and xylitol causes stomach upset in large amounts. Use the smallest amount that makes the dessert taste right, and skip dessert on weekdays if you are trying to lose weight. Sweet food keeps the craving alive.

## 1. Chocolate mousse

**Serves 4 · 20 minutes + 2 hours chilling · ~3g net carbs**

- 200ml double cream
- 50g 85% dark chocolate
- 1 tbsp cocoa powder
- 1 tsp vanilla
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tbsp erythritol (optional)

Melt the chocolate over a low heat, cool slightly, then fold into softly whipped cream with cocoa, vanilla and salt. Divide into glasses and chill. Get the [full chocolate mousse recipe]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-chocolate-mousse/) for the stabilising trick that stops it splitting.

## 2. No-bake lemon cheesecake

**Serves 10 · 20 minutes + 4 hours chilling · ~6g net carbs**

- **Base:** 100g ground almonds, 50g desiccated coconut, 60g melted butter, 1 tbsp erythritol
- **Filling:** 250g full-fat cream cheese, 250ml cream, 2 tbsp erythritol, zest and juice of 2 lemons
- **Top:** a few strawberries

Press the base into a lined 20cm tin and chill. Beat the cream cheese with sweetener and lemon, whip the cream separately and fold it through, then spread over the base. Chill for at least four hours. The lemon juice is doing the setting work, so do not reduce it.

## 3. Berry fool

**Serves 4 · 10 minutes · ~5g net carbs**

- 200ml cream
- 100g full-fat Greek yoghurt
- 100g raspberries or strawberries
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 tbsp erythritol (optional)

Crush half the berries with a fork, whip the cream with vanilla and sweetener, then fold in the yoghurt and crushed berries. Layer with the whole berries in glasses. This is the fastest dessert on the page and the least likely to spike anyone.

## 4. Banting brownies

**Makes 12 · 30 minutes · ~4g net carbs a square**

- 150g 85% dark chocolate
- 100g butter
- 3 eggs
- 80g almond flour
- 40g cocoa powder
- 60g erythritol
- 1 tsp baking powder
- Pinch of salt

Melt the chocolate and butter, cool, then whisk in the eggs and sweetener. Fold in almond flour, cocoa, baking powder and salt. Bake at 170°C for 20–22 minutes — pull them out while the centre is still slightly soft. Overbaking turns almond-flour brownies dry.

## 5. Malva pudding

**Serves 6 · 40 minutes · ~7g net carbs**

- 100g almond flour
- 30g coconut flour
- 60g erythritol
- 2 eggs
- 60g butter, melted
- 120ml full-cream milk
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 1 tsp vanilla
- **Sauce:** 100ml cream, 40g butter, 2 tbsp erythritol, 1 tsp vanilla

Mix the dry ingredients, whisk the wet, combine and bake at 180°C for 25–30 minutes. Warm the sauce ingredients and pour over the pudding while both are hot so it soaks in. Serve with cream. It is not the sugar bomb from childhood, but it fills the gap.

## 6. Banting milk tart

**Serves 8 · 30 minutes + 3 hours chilling · ~5g net carbs**

- **Crust:** 150g ground almonds, 50g butter, 1 tbsp erythritol, pinch of salt
- **Filling:** 500ml full-cream milk, 3 eggs, 3 tbsp erythritol, 1 tbsp psyllium husk (or 2 tsp gelatine powder), 1 tsp vanilla, cinnamon
- **Top:** ground cinnamon

Press the crust into a tart tin and bake at 180°C for 12–15 minutes. Heat the milk, whisk the eggs with sweetener and psyllium, temper the eggs with the hot milk, then return to the pan and stir over low heat until thick. If you are using gelatine instead, bloom it in 2 tbsp cold water and stir it into the hot custard off the heat. Pour into the crust, dust with cinnamon and chill. Psyllium thickens without the starch in cornflour — whisk it in well or it clumps, and expect a slightly softer set than a traditional milk tart.

## 7. Peppermint crisp tart

**Serves 8 · 20 minutes + 3 hours chilling · ~5g net carbs**

- 250ml cream
- 200g full-fat cream cheese
- 2 tbsp erythritol
- 1 tsp peppermint extract
- 50g 85% dark chocolate, grated
- 1 tbsp cocoa

Whip the cream, beat the cream cheese with sweetener and peppermint, fold together, then layer with grated chocolate. Chill and finish with cocoa. The original uses caramel treat and mint crisp chocolate; this version keeps the flavour and loses the sugar.

## 8. Banting rusks

**Makes 24 · 3 hours · ~2g net carbs a rusk**

Almond flour, coconut flour, psyllium, butter, eggs and cream, baked as a loaf, sliced and dried low and slow. Perfect with coffee and far better than a wheat rusk. Get the [Banting rusks recipe]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-rusks/).

## Dessert rules that actually help

1. **Sweeten less each time.** Your palate adjusts in two to three weeks.
2. **Use fat to carry the flavour.** Cream, butter, nuts and chocolate do more than sweetener.
3. **Portion before serving.** Put the rest in the fridge in a closed container.
4. **Berries are the fruit exception.** 50g raspberries or strawberries is about 3g net carbs.
5. **Do not trust "sugar-free".** Check total carbs and sugar alcohols on the label.

## Fruit on Banting

Raspberries and strawberries are the safest. Blueberries are fine in small portions. Bananas, grapes, mango, pineapple and dried fruit are high-carb — see [is banana banting friendly?]({{ site.baseurl }}/questions/is-banana-banting-friendly/) and [is honey banting friendly?]({{ site.baseurl }}/questions/is-honey-banting-friendly/).
