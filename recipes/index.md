# Banting recipes

**Short answer:** A Banting recipe keeps the carbs low, the fat natural and the ingredients recognisable. Most main meals here land under 10g net carbs a serving, use meat, fish, eggs, full-fat dairy and above-ground vegetables, and skip sugar, grains, seed oils and starchy vegetables.

## What makes a recipe Banting?

Banting is South Africa's version of low-carb, high-fat (LCHF) eating. [What is Banting?]({{ site.baseurl }}/guides/what-is-banting/) covers the full picture. The rules are simple enough to apply without an app:

1. **Keep net carbs low.** Most people aim for 25–50g net carbs a day. A main meal should sit around 5–10g.
2. **Use whole foods.** Meat, fish, eggs, cheese, cream, butter, olive oil, avocado, nuts and vegetables that grow above the ground.
3. **Skip sugar and refined starch.** No bread, rice, pasta, pap, potatoes, cereal, jam, honey or fruit juice.
4. **Add fat on purpose.** Butter on the veg, cream in the coffee, olive oil on the salad. Low-fat products usually replace fat with starch or sugar.
5. **Read the label.** "Glycaemic Carbohydrate" on a South African label is already net carbs. Check the ingredients for sugar, dextrose, maltodextrin and starch.

A recipe does not need special "Banting flour" to qualify. A braaied lamb chop with a green salad is a Banting recipe. So is a cheese omelette. The packaged substitutes — almond flour bread, seed crackers, sugar-free desserts — are there for when you want the texture, not because you need them.

## Net carb bands used on this site

| Band | Net carbs per serving | What it means |

| Low-carb | 0–10g | Eat freely, most meals should land here |

| High-carb | 20g+ | Treat as an occasional exception |

## The swaps that make any recipe Banting

| Instead of | Use | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| Wheat flour | Almond flour, coconut flour, psyllium husk | Almond flour is not 1:1; add psyllium for structure |
| Sugar | Erythritol, xylitol, stevia | Xylitol is toxic to dogs; go easy, it upsets stomachs |
| Rice | Cauliflower rice | Drain it well or the dish goes watery |
| Pasta | Courgette ribbons, cabbage noodles | Salt and drain courgette first |
| Potatoes | Cauliflower mash, roasted turnip | Add butter and cream, not milk powder |
| Bread | Almond flour loaf, seed loaf, lettuce wraps | Check shop-bought "Banting" bread for starch |
| Milk | Cream and water, unsweetened almond milk | Milk has ~5g carbs per 100ml |
| Chocolate | 85% dark chocolate, cocoa plus sweetener | Check the sugar line, not the cocoa percentage |

## Cooking Banting in a South African kitchen

The recipes here are written for what is actually on shelf at Checkers, Pick n Pay, Woolworths, Shoprite and Food Lover's Market: hake and snoek, boerewors, biltong, full-cream yoghurt, cheddar, cauliflower, baby marrows, cabbage and avo.

Two local label traps catch people out:

- **Boerewors** often contains rusk or cereal. Look for a butcher's blend with no filler, or ask.
- **"Banting" or "low-carb" packaged products** can still be 15–20g carbs a portion. Scan the barcode instead of trusting the front of the pack.

If you are cooking for a family, keep the starch separate rather than making two meals. Braai meat, roast chicken, mince and curry all work for everyone; the Banting part is the portion of veg and the absence of rice, pap or bread on your plate.

## About these recipes

Every recipe on this site was written for Banting Buddy. The methods are our own wording, not copied from a cookbook, a blog or another app, and the ingredient quantities are given in weights so you can check the carbs yourself.

A few things worth knowing before you cook:

- **Net carbs are estimates.** They come from ingredient labels and USDA FoodData Central. Your brand of yoghurt, almond flour or boerewors will shift the number.
- **Substitutions change the count.** Swapping psyllium for cornflour, or cream for milk, moves the result. The recipe notes say where it matters.
- **Label checks are part of the recipe.** Stock cubes, tinned tomatoes, boerewors, bacon, salami and "sugar-free" chocolate are the usual places where sugar, dextrose or starch hide.

If you spot something that does not fit Banting, tell us and we will fix it.

## Banting recipes FAQ

### Are Banting recipes keto?

Mostly, yes. Both are low-carb, high-fat. Banting allows a little more carbs — around 50g net a day versus 20–30g for strict keto — and leans on whole foods rather than macro percentages. A recipe under 10g net carbs works for both.

### Do I need almond flour to cook Banting?

No. The majority of Banting meals are meat, fish, eggs and vegetables cooked with butter or olive oil. Almond flour, coconut flour and psyllium are only needed for bread, pancakes, crackers and desserts.

### How many carbs should a Banting dinner have?

Aim for 5–10g net carbs for the main meal. That leaves room for breakfast, a snack and the fat in your coffee without pushing past 50g for the day.

### Can I eat Banting recipes at a braai?

Yes. Skip the rolls and pap, choose plain boerewors, chops, chicken or fish, and fill up on braaied vegetables and salad. See the [Banting braai recipes]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/braai/) for marinades and sides.
