# Banting braai recipes

**Short answer:** A braai is one of the easiest Banting meals — meat, fish and vegetables over coals, with no pap, rolls or sugary marinade. Stick to plain wors, chops, chicken, snoek and steak, add braaied veg and salad, and a normal braai lands at 3–8g net carbs.

The danger is not the meat. It is the marinade, the basting sauce, the wors filler and the pile of sides. Fix those four and you can eat at every braai this summer.

## 1. Boerewors — check the filler

**~2g net carbs per 100g (plain wors)**

Plain beef and pork wors should be meat, fat, salt, coriander, pepper and vinegar. Many supermarket wors recipes add rusk, cereal or sugar, which pushes it to 8–12g net carbs per 100g. Ask the butcher, or check the ingredients list. Braai it slowly over medium coals and do not prick it — you lose the fat that makes it good.

## 2. Lamb chops with garlic butter

**Serves 4 · 20 minutes · ~1g net carbs**

- 8 lamb chops
- 80g butter, softened
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tbsp chopped rosemary
- Salt, black pepper, lemon

Rub the chops with salt and pepper and braai over medium-high heat, 3–4 minutes a side for medium. Mash the butter with garlic and rosemary and put a spoon on each chop as it comes off the fire. Squeeze lemon over. Nothing else needed.

## 3. Espetada

**Serves 4 · 20 minutes + marinating · ~3g net carbs**

- 800g beef or ostrich fillet, cubed
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 bay leaves, crushed
- 1 tsp coarse salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 red pepper, cubed
- 1 onion, cut into wedges

Marinate the meat in oil, garlic, bay, salt and pepper for at least two hours. Thread onto skewers with pepper and onion and braai over hot coals, turning often, for 8–10 minutes. Ostrich is lean, so do not overcook it.

## 4. Snoek with lemon butter

**Serves 4 · 30 minutes · ~3g net carbs**

- 1 whole snoek, cleaned
- 100g butter
- 2 lemons
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt, pepper, parsley

Score the flesh, season inside and out, and braai in a hinged grid over medium coals for 20–25 minutes, turning once. Melt the butter with garlic and lemon and baste towards the end. Skip apricot jam — a tablespoon is about 12g sugar.

## 5. Chicken sosaties without sugar

**Serves 4 · 30 minutes + marinating · ~4g net carbs**

- 600g chicken thighs, cubed
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp mild curry powder
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 onion, cut into chunks
- 2 bay leaves

Marinate the chicken for 2–4 hours in oil, lemon, garlic and spices. Thread with onion and bay and braai over medium heat for 12–15 minutes, turning. Shop-bought sosatie marinade is usually sweetened; this one is not.

## 6. Steak with chimichurri

**Serves 4 · 20 minutes · ~2g net carbs**

- 4 sirloin or rump steaks
- **Chimichurri:** 1 cup parsley, 2 tbsp oregano, 3 cloves garlic, 120ml olive oil, 2 tbsp red wine vinegar, chilli flakes, salt

Season the steaks generously and braai over high heat, 3 minutes a side for medium-rare, then rest for 5 minutes. Blitz or chop the chimichurri ingredients and spoon over. The vinegar cuts the fat and makes the steak taste lighter.

## 7. Braaied mushrooms and courgettes

**Serves 4 · 15 minutes · ~3g net carbs**

- 400g portabellini mushrooms
- 2 baby marrows, halved lengthways
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- Salt, pepper, lemon, parsley

Toss the vegetables in oil, garlic, salt and pepper. Braai in a grid or on a plancha for 8–10 minutes, turning once. Finish with lemon and parsley. These are the side that stops you missing the potato salad.

## 8. Braai broodjie with seed bread

**Serves 2 · 10 minutes · ~5g net carbs**

- 4 slices [Banting seed loaf]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/bread/#3-seed-loaf) or almond bread
- Butter
- 60g cheddar, sliced
- 1 tomato, sliced
- 1 small onion, sliced
- Salt, pepper

Butter the outside of the bread, layer cheese, tomato and onion inside, and braai in a hinged grid over medium coals until the cheese melts and the bread crisps. Use thin slices or the bread will fall apart.

## Banting braai sides

- Big green salad with olive oil, lemon and salt
- [Cauliflower mash]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/dinner/#lamb-chops-with-cauliflower-mash) or cauliflower rice
- Braaied peppers, brinjal and mushrooms
- Cabbage slaw with a cream or olive-oil dressing
- Avo with salt and lemon
- [Banting-friendly braai sides]({{ site.baseurl }}/questions/banting-friendly-braai-sides/) for more options

## What to avoid at a braai

| Avoid | Why | Swap |
|---|---|---|
| Pap and stywe pap | ~30g net carbs a portion | Cauliflower mash |
| Rolls and garlic bread | 20g+ carbs | Seed bread, or none |
| Sweet chilli sauce | 20g+ sugar per 100g | Chimichurri, lemon butter |
| Apricot jam basting | 60g+ sugar per 100g | Lemon butter, olive oil |
| Potato salad | 15–20g carbs a scoop | Cauliflower or green salad |
| Beer and cider | 10–15g carbs a drink | Dry wine, soda water, brandy and water |

## A braai order that works

Start with biltong and olives while the fire burns. Put the wors on first, then the chops and sosaties. Add the vegetables when the coals are medium. Serve the steak last so it does not sit. For dessert, [chocolate mousse]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-chocolate-mousse/) made ahead and kept in the fridge.
