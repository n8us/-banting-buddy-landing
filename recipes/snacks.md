# Banting snacks

**Short answer:** The best Banting snacks are whole foods that need no preparation: biltong, cheese, boiled eggs, nuts, olives, cucumber and avocado. Most of the ideas below are under 2g net carbs, and none need a blender or a "low-carb" packet mix.

Snacking is not compulsory on Banting. If three meals keep you full, skip it. But a planned snack beats a garage pie at 4pm, especially in the first two weeks while your appetite adjusts.

## 1. Plain biltong

**~1g net carbs per 30g**

Beef or game biltong with visible fat is the best default. It has around 2–4g net carbs per 100g and roughly 50g protein. Avoid sweet chilli, BBQ and honey-glazed biltong, which can hit 12–18g per 100g. Read [is biltong banting friendly?]({{ site.baseurl }}/questions/is-biltong-banting-friendly/) for the label check.

## 2. Boiled eggs with salt

**~0.5g net carbs per egg**

Boil six on Sunday, keep them in the fridge, eat them with salt and black pepper. Cheap, portable and almost zero carb. A little olive-oil mayonnaise or butter makes them more filling.

## 3. Cheddar and apple? No — cheddar and cucumber

**~1g net carbs per 30g cheese**

30g mature cheddar with cucumber rounds. Cheese is low-carb but easy to overeat, so cut a portion rather than eating from the block. Avoid processed cheese spreads, which often contain starch and sugar.

## 4. Macadamia nuts

**~1.5g net carbs per 30g**

Macadamias are the lowest-carb nut, followed by pecans and walnuts. Portion them into a small container — a whole bag is 800+ calories and 12g+ carbs. Raw or dry-roasted, unsalted or lightly salted, no honey roasting.

## 5. Banting seed crackers with cream cheese

**~0.5g net carbs a cracker**

The closest thing to a cracker that still fits. Make a batch of [Banting seed crackers]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-seed-crackers/) and eat them with cream cheese, cheddar or avocado. Shop-bought "seed crackers" are often wheat-based; check the first three ingredients.

## 6. Avocado with salt and lemon

**~1.5g net carbs per half**

Half an avocado, flaky salt, lemon and a crack of pepper. Add a drizzle of olive oil or a spoon of cream cheese if you need it to go further. See [how many carbs in avocado]({{ site.baseurl }}/questions/how-many-carbs-in-avocado/).

## 7. Pork crackling

**0g net carbs**

Plain salted pork crackling is effectively zero carb. It is also easy to eat 500 calories without noticing. Buy the plain kind, not a flavoured snack with maltodextrin.

## 8. Olives

**~0.5g net carbs per 30g**

Green or black olives, drained, with a little olive oil and herbs. A good fat-based snack with almost no prep. Watch marinated olives with added sugar or honey.

## 9. Cucumber with cream cheese and chives

**~2g net carbs**

Cucumber rounds topped with full-fat cream cheese, chives and salt. This is the snack that replaces chips and dip. Add smoked salmon if you want it to feel like a meal.

## 10. Banting rusks with coffee

**~2g net carbs a rusk**

A [Banting rusk]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-rusks/) with black coffee or rooibos is the answer to the 10am tea break. Two rusks is a portion; the tin is not.

## 11. Chocolate mousse

**~3g net carbs a serving**

When the craving is sweet rather than salty, a small [Banting chocolate mousse]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-chocolate-mousse/) made with 85% chocolate and cream does the job. Keep it in the fridge so you are not tempted to drive to the shops.

## 12. Cheese and biltong plate

**~2g net carbs**

30g cheddar, 30g biltong, a few olives and cucumber. This is a snack that works as a light lunch, and it travels well in a cooler bag. Add a boiled egg for more protein.

## Snacks to avoid

| Snack | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Fruit (banana, apple, grapes) | 20g+ carbs a portion |
| Dried fruit and raisins | Concentrated sugar, 60g+ per 100g |
| Yoghurt cups and drinking yoghurt | 12–20g sugar a tub |
| Protein bars | Often 15–25g carbs; check the label |
| "Banting" crackers and rusks from a box | Can be 10–20g a portion |
| Biltong with sweet chilli or honey | 12–18g net carbs per 100g |
| Nuts and raisins mix | The raisins are the problem |
| Rice cakes and Provitas | Refined starch |

## How to snack without overdoing it

- **Plan the snack, not the impulse.** Keep two or three options in the fridge and one in your bag.
- **Pair protein with fat.** Biltong plus cheese, egg plus olive-oil mayo, cucumber plus cream cheese. Fat alone is easy to overeat.
- **Do not eat from the packet.** Portion into a bowl or container.
- **Drink water first.** Thirst and boredom both feel like hunger.
- **Log it.** A handful of nuts three times a day is 300+ calories and 10g+ carbs. The tracker in Banting Buddy makes that visible.

## Make your own snack box

A Sunday batch of [seed crackers]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-seed-crackers/), six boiled eggs, a portioned bag of macadamias and a pack of plain biltong covers the week. Add [Banting rusks]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-rusks/) if you drink tea, and a [chocolate mousse]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-chocolate-mousse/) in the fridge for the evening.
