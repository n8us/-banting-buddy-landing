# Banting bread recipes

**Short answer:** Banting bread replaces wheat flour with almond flour, coconut flour, psyllium husk, eggs and cheese. It does not rise like yeast bread and it does not taste like white bread — but it toasts, holds a sandwich and lands around 1–2g net carbs a slice.

The trick to low-carb bread is structure, not flavour. Almond flour alone is crumbly; psyllium husk gives it elasticity; eggs hold it together; a little acid (vinegar or lemon) stops it tasting eggy. Follow the ratios the first time, then adjust.

## Banting bread vs wheat bread

| Per slice | Wheat bread | Banting almond loaf |
|---|---|---|
| Net carbs | 12–15g | ~2g |
| Fibre | 1–2g | ~3g |
| Protein | 3g | ~6g |
| Fat | 1g | ~14g |
| Calories | ~80 | ~165 |

Banting bread is not a free food. It is calorie-dense and easy to overeat, so treat it as a swap for bread cravings rather than a daily staple.

## 1. Almond flour sandwich bread

**Makes 1 loaf (12 slices) · 1 hour · ~2g net carbs a slice**

The everyday loaf: 200g almond flour, 30g psyllium husk, 4 eggs, melted butter, water and a little vinegar. It slices thin, freezes well and makes a proper toasted cheese. Get the [full Banting bread recipe]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-bread/) with weights, method and troubleshooting.

## 2. Coconut flour bread

**Makes 1 loaf (10 slices) · 55 minutes · ~1.5g net carbs a slice**

- 80g coconut flour
- 6 eggs
- 60g melted butter or coconut oil
- 120ml full-cream milk or water
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tbsp psyllium husk

Coconut flour absorbs far more liquid than almond flour, which is why this loaf uses six eggs. Whisk the eggs until fluffy, add the melted fat and liquid, then fold in the dry ingredients. Bake at 175°C for 40–45 minutes. Let it cool completely before slicing or it will crumble.

## 3. Seed loaf

**Makes 1 loaf (12 slices) · 1 hour 10 minutes · ~1g net carbs a slice**

- 100g sunflower seeds
- 50g pumpkin seeds
- 50g flaxseeds
- 30g chia seeds
- 30g psyllium husk
- 4 eggs
- 250ml water
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt

Mix everything into a thick, wet dough, press into a lined loaf tin and bake at 180°C for 50–55 minutes. Cool, then slice thinly. This is the most "seedy" loaf and the best one for savoury toppings. It also makes excellent [seed crackers]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-seed-crackers/) if you roll it thinner.

## 4. Banting rusks

**Makes 24 · 3 hours · ~2g net carbs a rusk**

A buttermilk-style rusk made from almond flour, coconut flour, butter, eggs and cream, baked as a loaf then dried at low heat. The twice-baked method is what gives the crunch. Get the [Banting rusks recipe]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-rusks/).

## 5. Banting pizza base

**Makes 1 large base (8 slices) · 35 minutes · ~2g net carbs a slice**

Fathead dough: mozzarella, cream cheese, egg and almond flour. It stretches, rolls and crisps, and it is the closest thing to a real pizza base in low-carb cooking. Get the [Banting pizza base recipe]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-pizza-base/).

## 6. Banting hot cross buns

**Makes 12 · 2 hours · ~3g net carbs a bun**

- 200g almond flour
- 40g coconut flour
- 40g psyllium husk
- 3 eggs
- 60g butter, softened
- 150ml warm water
- 1 tbsp erythritol
- 2 tsp mixed spice
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp baking powder
- Pinch of salt

Mix into a sticky dough, shape 12 buns, and bake at 180°C for 25–30 minutes. Pipe a cross with cream cheese thinned with a little water. They will not be light and fluffy like yeast buns; they are dense, spicy and good toasted with butter.

## Why low-carb bread fails (and how to fix it)

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Crumbly slices | Too little binder | Add 1 tbsp psyllium or 1 egg |
| Wet centre | Coconut flour or watery veg | Bake longer at a lower temperature |
| Tastes eggy | Too many eggs, no acid | Add 1 tsp vinegar or lemon juice |
| Flat loaf | No structure | Psyllium husk is not optional in almond loaves |
| Green/grey tinge | Sunflower seeds reacting to baking soda | Use baking powder, or skip the soda |

## Storing and freezing

- **Counter:** 2 days in an airtight container, sliced.
- **Fridge:** up to 5 days, but it dries out — toast before eating.
- **Freezer:** slice first, freeze flat, then toast from frozen. This is the best method; a loaf lasts two months.
- **Reviving:** sprinkle with water and toast, or fry in butter.

## What to put on Banting bread

Butter, cream cheese, avocado, cheddar, smoked salmon, egg mayo, biltong, liver pâté, or a toasted cheese with tomato. Avoid jam, honey, syrup and sweet spreads — that is where the carbs come back.
