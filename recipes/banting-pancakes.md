# Banting pancakes recipe

**Short answer:** Banting pancakes are made from eggs and cream cheese, with a little psyllium for structure. They take 15 minutes, need no flour, and land at about **2g net carbs a serving**. They are softer and tangier than wheat pancakes, and they hold butter and berries well.

This is the recipe to make on a Sunday morning when the family is having real pancakes. The texture is closer to a thin crumpet than a fluffy American pancake, but it does the job.

| | |
|---|---|
| Prep time | 5 minutes |
| Cook time | 10 minutes |
| Yield | 6 small pancakes (2 servings) |
| Net carbs | ~2g a serving |
| Equipment | Non-stick pan, whisk or blender |

## Ingredients

- 2 large eggs
- 60g full-fat cream cheese, softened
- 1 tsp psyllium husk (optional but helps them hold together)
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp erythritol (optional)
- Pinch of salt
- Butter or coconut oil for frying

## Method

1. **Blend the batter.** Put the eggs, cream cheese, psyllium, cinnamon, sweetener and salt in a blender or jug and whisk until completely smooth. Any lumps of cream cheese will burn in the pan.
2. **Rest for 5 minutes.** The psyllium thickens the batter so the pancakes hold their shape.
3. **Heat the pan.** Medium-low heat with a knob of butter. If the pan is too hot, the outside burns before the middle sets.
4. **Cook.** Pour 2 tbsp of batter per pancake. Cook for 2 minutes until the edges look set and bubbles appear, then flip gently and cook for 1 minute more.
5. **Keep warm.** Stack on a plate under a clean tea towel while you cook the rest.

## Toppings that stay Banting

- Butter and a few raspberries or strawberries (about 3g net carbs per 50g)
- Whipped cream with cinnamon
- Cream cheese and chopped pecans
- 85% dark chocolate, melted
- Sugar-free berry compote: 100g berries simmered with 1 tbsp erythritol and a squeeze of lemon

Avoid maple syrup, honey, jam and banana. A tablespoon of maple syrup is about 13g sugar — more than the pancakes themselves.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Pancakes fall apart | Add 1 tsp psyllium and rest the batter longer |
| Rubbery texture | Too much cream cheese; use 50g instead of 60g |
| Burned outside, raw inside | Lower the heat and cover the pan for the first minute |
| Sticking | More butter, and do not flip until the edges are set |

## Variations

- **Savoury:** skip the cinnamon and sweetener, add 1 tbsp chopped chives and serve with smoked salmon.
- **Cheesy:** fold 30g grated cheddar into the batter.
- **Cocoa:** add 1 tsp cocoa powder and a little extra sweetener.
- **Larger batch:** the recipe doubles cleanly; cook once and reheat in a toaster.

## Storage

Keep leftover pancakes in the fridge for 3 days and reheat in a dry pan or toaster. They freeze well between sheets of baking paper for up to a month.

## FAQ

### How many carbs are in Banting pancakes?

About 2g net carbs per serving (three small pancakes), almost all from the cream cheese and eggs. Toppings are what push the number up.

### Can I make Banting pancakes without cream cheese?

Yes. Use 60g full-fat Greek yoghurt or 50g ricotta. The texture is slightly softer and the carb count is similar.

### Are these pancakes keto?

Yes. At roughly 2g net carbs a serving they fit keto as well as Banting.
