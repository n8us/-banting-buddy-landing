# Banting dinner recipes

**Short answer:** A Banting dinner is a palm-sized portion of protein, a thumb of fat and as much above-ground vegetables as you want. The ten recipes below are normal South African suppers with the starch removed — most land between 4g and 9g net carbs a serving.

Cook one of these and keep a portion for lunch. Leftovers are the difference between staying on plan and reaching for bread at 3pm.

## 1. Banting bobotie {#banting-bobotie}

**Serves 4 · 50 minutes · ~7g net carbs a serving**

Bobotie without the sugar and without the rice. The egg custard on top is what makes it.

- 500g beef mince
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp apple cider vinegar (or lemon juice)
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 2 eggs
- 100ml cream
- 2 bay leaves
- Salt, pepper, butter

Bobotie normally gets its tang from fruit chutney, which is mostly sugar and dried fruit. Vinegar, lemon and ground coriander give the same sweet-sour lift without the carbs.

Fry the onion in butter, add the mince and spices and cook until browned. Stir in tomato paste, vinegar and ground coriander, season, and press into a baking dish. Whisk the eggs with cream, pour over, tuck in bay leaves and bake at 180°C for 25–30 minutes until set and golden. Serve with [cauliflower rice]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/dinner/#chicken-curry-with-cauliflower-rice) or a green salad.

## 2. Chicken curry with cauliflower rice {#chicken-curry-with-cauliflower-rice}

**Serves 4 · 35 minutes · ~7g net carbs a serving**

- 600g chicken thighs, cubed
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 tbsp curry powder
- 1 tin (400ml) coconut cream
- 1 cup chicken stock (homemade, or a cube with no added sugar or starch)
- 2 cups cauliflower rice
- 2 tbsp butter or coconut oil
- Salt, pepper, fresh coriander

Brown the chicken in batches, remove, then soften the onion and toast the curry powder for 30 seconds. Return the chicken with coconut cream and stock and simmer for 20 minutes. Meanwhile fry the cauliflower rice in butter for 5–6 minutes — do not crowd the pan or it steams. Season and serve with coriander.

## 3. Hake with lemon butter and broccoli {#hake-with-lemon-butter-and-broccoli}

**Serves 2 · 20 minutes · ~4g net carbs a serving**

- 2 hake fillets (about 150g each)
- 60g butter
- 1 lemon, zest and juice
- 1 head broccoli
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, white pepper, parsley

Steam or roast the broccoli. Pat the hake dry, season, and pan-fry in olive oil for 3–4 minutes a side. Melt the butter with lemon zest and juice, spoon over the fish and finish with parsley. Hake is cheap, mild and takes less time than the broccoli.

## 4. Boerewors with cabbage

**Serves 4 · 25 minutes · ~5g net carbs a serving**

- 500g boerewors with no rusk or cereal filler
- 1 small cabbage, shredded
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tsp caraway seeds (optional)
- Salt, pepper, vinegar

Grill or braai the wors until cooked through. Fry the cabbage in butter until soft at the edges, splash with vinegar and season. Check the wors ingredients: cereal, rusk and sugar push the carbs up fast.

## 5. Beef mince with courgette "pasta"

**Serves 4 · 30 minutes · ~7g net carbs a serving**

- 500g beef mince
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (check for added sugar)
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 3 baby marrows, ribboned
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Parmesan, basil, salt, pepper

Brown the mince, add onion and garlic, then tomatoes and paste, and simmer for 15 minutes. Salt the courgette ribbons and leave them in a colander for 10 minutes, squeeze dry, then toss in a hot pan for 2 minutes. Serve the sauce on top with parmesan. Do not boil the courgette or it turns to mush.

## 6. Lamb chops with cauliflower mash {#lamb-chops-with-cauliflower-mash}

**Serves 4 · 30 minutes · ~6g net carbs a serving**

- 8 lamb chops
- 1 head cauliflower
- 60g butter
- 60ml cream
- 3 cloves garlic
- Rosemary, salt, pepper, olive oil

Roast or pan-fry the chops with rosemary and garlic. Boil the cauliflower until soft, drain very well, then blitz with butter and cream until smooth. Keep the heat low when adding cream or it splits. Season generously — cauliflower needs more salt than potato.

## 7. Chicken and spinach bake

**Serves 4 · 40 minutes · ~5g net carbs a serving**

- 6 chicken breasts
- 400g spinach
- 150g cream cheese
- 100g mozzarella, grated
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper, oregano

Brown the chicken in olive oil and season. Wilt the spinach with garlic, squeeze out the water, then stir in the cream cheese. Put the spinach in a baking dish, top with chicken and mozzarella, and bake at 180°C for 20–25 minutes. A weeknight dish that also reheats well for lunch.

## 8. Snoek with lemon butter

**Serves 4 · 30 minutes · ~3g net carbs a serving**

- 1 whole snoek, cleaned (about 1kg)
- 100g butter
- 2 lemons
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt, pepper, parsley

Score the snoek, rub with salt, pepper and paprika, and braai or bake at 200°C for 20–25 minutes. Melt the butter with garlic and lemon juice and pour over before serving. Skip the apricot jam glaze — it is mostly sugar.

## 9. Pork chops with green beans

**Serves 4 · 25 minutes · ~5g net carbs a serving**

- 4 pork chops
- 300g green beans
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
- 100ml cream
- Salt, pepper, sage

Pan-fry the chops in butter and rest them. Add cream and mustard to the pan, scrape up the browned bits and simmer for 2 minutes. Steam the beans and toss with butter. The sauce is the whole dish — do not skip it.

## 10. Banting pizza

**Serves 4 · 35 minutes · ~12g net carbs a serving**

A fathead dough base made from mozzarella, cream cheese, egg and almond flour, topped with tomato, cheese and Banting-friendly toppings. It is a medium-carb recipe, not an everyday one, but it stops the takeaway order. Get the [Banting pizza base recipe]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-pizza-base/).

## Sides that keep dinner low-carb

- Cauliflower rice, mash or roasted florets
- Cabbage fried in butter
- Green beans, broccoli, spinach, courgette
- Big green salad with olive oil and lemon
- Roasted peppers, mushrooms and brinjal
- Avo with salt and lemon

## What to leave off the plate

Rice, pap, pasta, potatoes, bread, roti, couscous and sweet potato. If the family wants starch, cook it separately and keep your portion of veg and protein generous. You do not need a second meal — just an extra vegetable.

## Use the leftovers

Cook double on Sunday and Monday. Bobotie, curry, chicken bake and mince sauce all taste better the next day, and having lunch ready removes the main reason people fall off plan.
