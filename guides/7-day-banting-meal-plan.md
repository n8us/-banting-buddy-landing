# 7-Day Banting Meal Plan That Works

Each day below stays near 30 to 45g net carbs. (Remember: on South African store labels, "Glycaemic Carbohydrate" is already net carbs). You get eggs or plain full-cream yoghurt for breakfast, quality protein with above-ground veg for lunch and supper, plus healthy fats to keep you full.

Drink water, rooibos, herbal tea, or black coffee (or with a splash of cream). Salt your food generously — when you cut carbs, your kidneys shed sodium faster. If you take medication for diabetes or blood pressure, talk to your doctor before changing how you eat.

## The week at a glance

| Day | Breakfast | Lunch | Supper | Est. net carbs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | 2 eggs fried in butter, 100g spinach | Tinned pilchards (in brine or olive oil) 120g + olive oil mayo, salad | Beef mince 150g, fried cabbage in butter | 32g |

| Fri | Coconut porridge, almond flour 30g + cream | Snoek 150g, coleslaw with olive oil dressing | Chicken curry, coconut cream, green beans | 40g |

Totals are estimates from USDA FoodData Central, accessed Sep 2026, plus SA labels where noted. Your brands will shift them by a few grams.

You do not need to weigh forever. Weigh for three days, then eyeball. Most people overeat nuts and undercount milk in coffee. Those two fixes solve half the stalls I hear about.

## Shopping once for the week

Meat and fish: eggs 18, beef mince 500g, hake 300g, chicken thighs 600g, clean boerewors 400g (check ingredients for zero cereal or rusk), tinned pilchards and tuna in brine/oil, snoek 300g, bacon 200g (no sugar cure), biltong 100g. Dairy: real butter, fresh cream 250ml, cheddar 200g, plain full-cream yoghurt 1kg. Veg and fruit: spinach, cabbage, cauliflower, broccoli, salad leaves, cucumber, peppers, onions, mushrooms, tomatoes, avo x3, lemons, frozen berries. Pantry: extra virgin olive oil, coconut cream, almond flour, chia, salt, pepper, curry powder, and avocado-oil mayo (or make your own with egg yolks and olive oil to skip industrial sunflower seed oils).

That trolley cost me about R950 at Pick n Pay in Sep 2026, Kensington Cape Town. Prices move by store, but it gives you a base. Woolies ran about R200 more for the same list when I priced it for fun.

## Swaps when you get bored

Boredom kills meal plans faster than hunger. You can swap any lunch with any other lunch. Keep the portion of protein near palm size plus a thumb of fat, then fill the plate with green veg.

No fish? Use eggs or leftover braai meat. No cream? Use butter or olive oil. Craving bread? Fry a wrap from the [banting flour and sweetener swaps]({{ site.baseurl }}/compare/banting-flour-sweetener-swaps/) page, but keep it to one. Need snack ideas? The [banting-friendly snacks in South Africa]({{ site.baseurl }}/questions/banting-friendly-snacks-south-africa/) list has portions that fit this plan.
