# Banting meal plan: a free 7-day plan

**Short answer:** This is a free 7-day Banting meal plan built around normal South African food — eggs, mince, hake, chicken, boerewors, cabbage and cauliflower. Each day lands around **30–40g net carbs**, with a single shopping list and swaps for when you get bored.

Each day below stays near 30 to 40g net carbs. (Remember: on South African store labels, "Glycaemic Carbohydrate" is already net carbs). You get eggs or plain full-cream yoghurt for breakfast, quality protein with above-ground veg for lunch and supper, plus healthy fats to keep you full.

Drink water, rooibos, herbal tea, or black coffee (or with a splash of cream). Salt your food generously — when you cut carbs, your kidneys shed sodium faster. If you take medication for diabetes or blood pressure, talk to your doctor before changing how you eat.

## Portion sizes that fit this plan

You do not need a scale for the week. Use your hand:

| Food | Portion | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Meat, fish or chicken | 120–180g | Palm, thickness of your hand |
| Eggs | 2–3 | — |
| Cheese | 30–50g | Two thumb-sized pieces |
| Nuts | 20–30g | Small handful |
| Butter or olive oil | 1–2 tbsp | Thumb |
| Vegetables | 2 cups+ | Half the plate |
| Berries | 50g | Small handful |

If you are hungry between meals, add protein and vegetables before adding nuts or cheese. Those are the two easiest things to overeat on Banting.

## Shopping once for the week

Meat and fish: eggs 18, beef mince 500g, hake 300g, chicken thighs 600g, clean boerewors 400g (check ingredients for zero cereal or rusk), tinned pilchards and tuna in brine/oil, snoek 300g, bacon 200g (no sugar cure), biltong 100g. Dairy: real butter, fresh cream 250ml, cheddar 200g, plain full-cream yoghurt 1kg. Veg and fruit: spinach, cabbage, cauliflower, broccoli, salad leaves, cucumber, peppers, onions, mushrooms, tomatoes, avo x3, lemons, frozen berries. Pantry: extra virgin olive oil, coconut cream, almond flour, chia, salt, pepper, curry powder, and avocado-oil mayo (or make your own with egg yolks and olive oil to skip industrial sunflower seed oils).

## Swaps when you get bored

Boredom kills meal plans faster than hunger. You can swap any lunch with any other lunch. Keep the portion of protein near palm size plus a thumb of fat, then fill the plate with green veg.

No fish? Use eggs or leftover braai meat. No cream? Use butter or olive oil. Craving bread? Fry a wrap from the [banting flour and sweetener swaps]({{ site.baseurl }}/compare/banting-flour-sweetener-swaps/) page, but keep it to one. Need snack ideas? The [banting-friendly snacks in South Africa]({{ site.baseurl }}/questions/banting-friendly-snacks-south-africa/) list has portions that fit this plan.

## Prep once, eat all week

A 90-minute Sunday session makes the plan easy to follow:

1. Boil 6–8 eggs for breakfasts and snacks.
2. Brown 500g mince and keep it plain, or turn half into the bobotie filling.
3. Roast a tray of chicken thighs and a tray of vegetables.
4. Make a jar of olive-oil dressing: 6 tbsp olive oil, 2 tbsp vinegar, 1 tsp mustard, salt, pepper.
5. Wash and chop salad vegetables into containers.
6. Bake a [Banting bread]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-bread/) or a batch of [seed crackers]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/banting-seed-crackers/) for snacks.

Cook double at dinner on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Those three lunches are already in the plan as leftovers.

## Banting meal plan FAQ

### Is this 7-day plan keto?

No, it is Banting. At 30–40g net carbs a day it is slightly higher than strict keto (20–30g), which leaves room for yoghurt, berries and nuts. Every meal is still keto-compatible if you reduce the fruit and dairy portions.

### Can I repeat the same day twice?

Yes. Most people repeat breakfast and lunch and vary dinner. The plan is a template, not a rule.

### What if I don't eat fish?

Replace hake and snoek with chicken thighs, eggs, mince or boerewors. Keep the portion the same. See the [Banting dinner recipes]({{ site.baseurl }}/recipes/dinner/) for alternatives.

### Can I follow this plan vegetarian?

Yes, but you need to replace the meat with eggs, cheese, tofu or tempeh, and watch the carbs in legumes. The app's recipe library includes vegetarian options.
