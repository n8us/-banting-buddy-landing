# Banting on a Budget in South Africa

Base your week on eggs, chicken thighs, mince, tinned fish, cabbage, and spinach, and skip fancy steaks and imported "keto" snacks. Basic braai meat, eggs, and local green veg keep costs down. (Remember: on South African food labels, "Glycaemic Carbohydrate" already gives you net carbs — check separately for sugar alcohols.)

## Cheap proteins ranked

Cheapest first, roughly: eggs, chicken thighs with skin, tinned pilchards, beef mince, pork rashers. Carb figures from USDA FoodData Central entries for each food (see sources at the end).

Cheapest days are eggs, thighs and pilchards. Mince twice a week keeps flavour up without breaking the budget. Biltong is a treat snack, not a base meat.

## A one-week trolley

Eggs (a large pack), chicken thighs, beef mince, tinned pilchards and tuna, bacon without sugar cure, butter, cream, cheddar, cabbage, spinach, cauliflower, salad leaves, avo, peanuts. House brands of butter and cheese are usually cheaper than name brands for the same trolley.

Cook once, eat twice. Roast thighs Sunday, eat cold Monday lunch. Brown 1kg mince with cabbage Tuesday, pack half for Wednesday. Boil six eggs for grab snacks. Soup from bones and leftover veg stretches to Friday.

Veg to favour: cabbage, spinach, gem squash, green beans when on special, frozen cauli rice when fresh is dear. Fruit: stick to half an avo or a handful of frozen berries. A banana looks cheap, but at 23.8g net per medium fruit (USDA: 22.8g total, 2.6g fibre per 100g) it blows your day.

## Store habits that save Rands

Shop with a list from the [7-day Banting meal plan]({{ site.baseurl }}/guides/7-day-banting-meal-plan/). It was built to reuse leftovers.

Check the meat markdown fridge first. I got pork chops at 30% off at 9am at Checkers, froze them the same day. Tinned fish in brine or oil beats tomato sauce versions with added sugar. Plain full-cream yoghurt in 1kg tubs costs half per 100g of little flavoured cups.

Woolworths is great for quality but brutal for budget Banting. I buy butter, cream and bulk nuts at Checkers or Pick n Pay, then only grab marked-down veg at Woolies if I pass one. Your favourite store may differ, prices shift by suburb.

Skip Banting-branded bars, breads and crackers till your budget allows. One loaf with 3g net per slice is fine as a treat, but eggs fill you more.
