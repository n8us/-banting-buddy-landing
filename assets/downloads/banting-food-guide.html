🧭 The 3 Banting Buddy Journey Phases
🔍 Discovery
1–2 Weeks
  • Preparation phase — no strict rules yet.
  • Eat from all food lists while learning the ropes.
  • Clean out cupboards & finish non-green items to avoid waste.
  • Focus new grocery shopping on Green items to get ready for Active Loss.
🔥 Active Loss
1–3 Months / Goal
  • The strict weight-loss phase — where the real fat burning occurs.
  • Eat ONLY from the Green list.
  • Body enters nutritional fat adaptation (ketosis).
  • Track macros & stay disciplined.
✅ Maintenance
Lifelong
  • Goal weight reached — congratulations!
  • Gradually reintroduce some Orange list foods in controlled portions.
  • Sustainable, lifelong way of eating.
  • If weight creeps up, briefly return to Active Loss to reset.

🍸 Alcohol Guidance Across Phases

During the Discovery and Active Loss phases, avoid alcohol completely (alcohol halts liver fat burning). In the Maintenance phase, you may choose zero-carb distilled 40% spirits (gin, vodka, whisky) with sugar-free mixers (plain soda water with fresh lime/lemon), dry red/white wines, or Brut Champagne in moderation. Full details are in the Grey Area.

🟢 GREEN LIST Eat to Hunger • 0 to 5g Carbs / 100g
The foundation of fat adaptation. Eat freely whenever hungry without calorie restriction until comfortably satisfied.
🥬 Leafy Greens & Low-Carb Vegetables
All green leafy vegetables (spinach, kale, Swiss chard, rocket, watercress, romaine, endive, radicchio), artichoke hearts, asparagus, aubergine (eggplant), avocado, broccoli, Brussels sprouts, cabbage, cauliflower, celery, courgettes (zucchini / baby marrow), cucumber, fennel, garlic, gem squash, leeks, mushrooms (all varieties), okra, olives, onions, shallots, spring onions, peppers (all kinds), radishes, rhubarb, sugar-snap peas, mange-tout, tomatoes, turnips.
🥩 Clean Animal Proteins & Eggs
All meats, poultry, and game (beef, lamb, pork, chicken, turkey, duck, venison), all organ meats (beef/chicken liver, kidney, heart), all wild and fresh seafood (salmon, mackerel, sardines, tuna, trout, prawns, calamari, oysters, mussels), pasture-raised eggs (whole), naturally cured meats with zero sugar (bacon, biltong, pancetta, prosciutto, sugar-free salami, parma ham).
🧈 Wholesome Natural Fats & Cooking Oils
Farm butter, ghee, extra virgin olive oil, cold-pressed avocado oil, extra virgin coconut oil, macadamia nut oil, rendered animal fats (beef tallow, duck fat, lard, bacon fat), firm & aged cheeses (cheddar, parmesan, gouda, pecorino, emmental), raw seeds (flax, chia, pumpkin, sesame, sunflower).
🥣 Fermented Foods & Gut Support
Homemade bone broths, raw sauerkraut, authentic kimchi, naturally fermented sour pickles (sugar-free brine), unsweetened coconut yogurt, milk kefir, coconut kefir.
☕ Drinks & Pantry Condiments
Pure still & sparkling water, caffeine-free herbal teas (peppermint, chamomile, rooibos), all raw vinegars (apple cider vinegar, wine vinegars with no sugar), tamari (fermented gluten-free soy sauce), natural herbs & spices, sugar-free avocado-oil mayonnaise.
🟠 ORANGE LIST Exercise Self-Control • 6 to 25g Carbs / 100g
Nutritious whole foods with higher carbohydrate density. Reintroduce during Maintenance or keep to small, disciplined portions (e.g. 1 closed handful daily).
🧀 Whole Dairy (Full-Fat & Unpasteurized) — Limit: ~¼ cup daily
Soft cheeses (brie, camembert, feta, fresh mozzarella, ricotta, gorgonzola, blue cheese), cottage cheese, heavy cream, cream cheese, sour cream, crème fraîche, full-fat Greek yogurt, whole cow's milk (small splash), unsweetened almond/coconut milk.
🥕 Nutrient-Rich Root Vegetables — Limit: Max ½ closed handful
Beetroot, golden beets, butternut squash, pumpkin, carrots, celeriac, jicama, parsnips, rutabagas, spaghetti squash, sweet potatoes, green garden peas.
🍓 Fresh Fruits — Limit: 1 small closed handful (post-meal)
Best Choice: Blackberries, blueberries, raspberries, strawberries, gooseberries.
Higher Sugar: Apples, apricots, bananas, cherries, fresh figs, granadilla (passionfruit), grapes, guavas, kiwi, mangoes, oranges, clementines, peaches, nectarines, pears, plums, pomegranates, watermelon, papaya, pineapple.
🥜 Raw Nuts & Sugar-Free Nut Butters — Limit: 1 closed handful (~2 tbsp)
Raw almonds, macadamias, walnuts, pecans, hazelnuts, Brazil nuts, pistachios, raw cashews, chestnuts, 100% natural sugar-free nut butters.
🫘 Soaked Legumes & Pulses (Maintenance Only)
Chickpeas, lentils, kidney beans, cannellini beans, black-eyed beans, edamame, whole raw peanuts in shells.
🍵 Caffeinated Beverages & Live Drinks
Fresh black coffee, espresso, black tea, green tea, oolong tea, plain water kefir, low-sugar kombucha.
🪸 CORAL LIST Hardly Ever • Maintenance Phase Only
Gluten-free and unrefined, but carries a high glycemic/carb load. Strictly excluded during Active Loss; enjoyed only as occasional transition foods at goal weight.
🌾 Gluten-Free Whole Grains & Seeds
Amaranth, arrowroot, buckwheat, certified gluten-free oats, millet, quinoa, sorghum, teff, tapioca, brown rice, wild rice, pure rice noodles, plain air-popped popcorn.
🥣 Alternative Flours (Occasional Baking)
Almond flour, coconut flour, chickpea flour, pea flour, corn flour, stoneground polenta (maize meal).
🍫 Whole-Food Indulgences & Sweeteners
Dark chocolate (85%+ cocoa minimum), fresh medjool dates, prunes, dried unsweetened fruit, raw unpasteurized honey, 100% pure maple syrup (micro-amounts for cooking).
🥤 Fresh Smoothies & Pure Vegetable Juices
Whole vegetable juices (without added fruit juice), plain Greek yogurt or berry smoothies (no ice cream or commercial syrups).
⚪ GREY AREA Your Decision • Mindful Lifestyle Choices
Modern low-carb adaptations and conscious indulgences. Best applied during Maintenance to keep life enjoyable and sustainable.
🧁 Low-Carb Baked Goods (Made with Compliant Ingredients)
Almond flour bread, coconut flour pancakes/waffles, seed crackers and crispbreads, Banting rusks, low-carb pizza bases (fathead or cauliflower), erythritol/stevia-sweetened cheesecakes and brownies.
🌿 Clean Non-Nutritive Sweeteners
Pure erythritol, stevia leaf extract, monk fruit extract, xylitol (keep away from pets—toxic to dogs). Avoid artificial sweeteners like aspartame or acesulfame-K.
🍸 Mindful Alcohol (Maintenance Phase Only)
Best Choice (Zero Carb): 40% distilled spirits (gin, vodka, whisky, tequila, rum, brandy) served neat or with plain soda water + fresh lemon/lime.
Wine & Bubbles: Dry red wine, dry white wine, brut Champagne / dry sparkling wine.
Strictly Avoid: Beer, cider, sweet dessert wines, sugary liqueurs, and soda/tonic mixers.
🌱 Clean Plant Protein Supplements
Unflavored pea protein isolate, egg white protein powder, naturally fermented tempeh or non-GMO organic firm tofu.
🔴 RED LIST Never Ever • Completely Excluded
Inflammatory, insulin-spiking, highly processed foods. Completely eliminate these to restore metabolic health and keep weight off permanently.
🍬 Refined Sugars & Sweeteners
White/brown/icing sugar, high-fructose corn syrup, agave nectar, glucose, fructose, cordials, commercial syrups, standard candy, commercial milk chocolate, jams, commercial ice cream.
🌾 Gluten-Containing Grains & Modern Wheat
All wheat flours, commercial breads, rolls, croissants, pastries, barley, bulgur, couscous, durum, rye, semolina, spelt, triticale, commercial wheat pastas, pizza crusts.
🥣 Boxed Cereals & Packaged Snacks
Commercial breakfast cereals (corn flakes, muesli, granola, instant porridge), potato crisps/chips, commercial crackers, pretzels, breaded or battered foods (nuggets, battered fish).
🌻 Industrial Seed & Hydrogenated Oils
Canola, sunflower, corn, cottonseed, soybean, safflower, rice bran oils, margarine, shortening, commercial salad dressings and sauces made with seed oils.
🌭 Ultra-Processed Meats & Artificial Dairy
Polony, hot dogs, commercial vienna sausages with cereal fillers, sugar-cured meats, coffee creamers, condensed milk, commercial cheese spreads, processed cheese slices.
🥤 Sugary & Chemical Drinks
Regular fizzy sodas, diet sodas with aspartame/acesulfame-K, commercial fruit juices, commercial iced teas, energy drinks, flavored milks and milkshakes.
