Eggsany style

ORANGE6–25g net carbs /100g · one measured portion

Root vegbeetroot, butternut, pumpkin, carrot, celeriac, parsnip, sweet potato, peas, spaghetti squash

Drinkscoffee, black tea, green tea, oolong, low-sugar kombucha, water kefir

CORAL26g+ whole food · hardly ever

Gluten-free grainsbrown, wild and jasmine rice, rice noodles, quinoa, buckwheat, millet, sorghum, teff, amaranth, arrowroot, tapioca, GF oats, air-popped popcorn
Alternative floursalmond, coconut, chickpea, pea, corn flour, polenta
Natural sugarsraw honey, maple syrup, dates, prunes, unsweetened dried fruit, dark chocolate 85%+
Whole-food drinksvegetable juice with no fruit juice, plain yoghurt or berry smoothie, no syrup
NoteMaintenance only. Excluded during active weight loss.

GREYnot a carb band · your call

Low-carb bakesalmond flour bread, coconut pancakes, seed crackers, rusks, low-carb pizza base, erythritol cheesecake and brownies
Sweetenerserythritol, stevia, monk fruit, xylitol (toxic to dogs)
Alcohol, maintenancegin, vodka, whisky, tequila, rum, brandy with soda and lime; dry wine; brut champagne

Avoidaspartame, acesulfame-K, beer, cider, sweet mixers
